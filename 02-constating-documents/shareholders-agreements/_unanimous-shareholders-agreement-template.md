## Article 1 – Interpretation

In this Agreement:

- **"Act"** means the Canada Business Corporations Act, R.S.C. 1985, c. C-44, as amended.
- **"Affiliate"** means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person.
- **"Board"** means the board of directors of the Corporation.
- **"Business Day"** means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or statutory holiday in [PROVINCE].
- **"Change of Control"** means (a) a sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Corporation; (b) a merger, amalgamation, or other business combination in which the shareholders of the Corporation immediately prior thereto do not hold a majority of the voting power of the surviving entity; or (c) a sale or transfer of shares representing more than 50% of the voting power of the Corporation to a third party.
- **"Common Shares"** means the common shares in the capital of the Corporation.
- **"Confidential Information"** has the meaning set forth in Section 9.1.
- **"Drag-Along Notice"** has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.
- **"Equity Securities"** means Common Shares, any other shares, or any securities convertible into or exchangeable for shares.
- **"Fair Market Value"** means the fair market value of the shares as determined in good faith by the Board, or if disputed, by an independent valuator selected pursuant to Section 4.7.
- **"Family Member"** means, with respect to any individual, such individual's spouse, common-law partner, parents, siblings, children, and grandchildren.
- **"Founder"** means [FOUNDER NAME].
- **"Investor"** means any Person who acquires Equity Securities from the Corporation in a Qualified Financing.
- **"IPO"** means an initial public offering of shares of the Corporation on a recognized stock exchange.
- **"Majority Shareholders"** means Shareholders holding, in aggregate, more than 50% of the issued and outstanding shares.
- **"New Securities"** has the meaning set forth in Section 6.1.
- **"Permitted Transfer"** has the meaning set forth in Section 4.4.
- **"Person"** means an individual, corporation, partnership, trust, or other entity.
- **"Pro Rata Share"** means, with respect to any Shareholder, the percentage of the total issued and outstanding shares held by such Shareholder.
- **"Qualified Financing"** means the issuance and sale of Equity Securities to one or more Investors resulting in aggregate gross proceeds to the Corporation of at least CAD $[QUALIFIED FINANCING THRESHOLD].

## Article 2 – Governance

### 2.1 Board Composition

(a) The Board shall consist of a minimum of [MINIMUM DIRECTORS] and a maximum of [MAXIMUM DIRECTORS] directors.

(b) As of the date hereof, the Board shall consist of [NUMBER] director(s), being [INITIAL DIRECTOR NAME(S)].

(c) The Founder, for so long as they hold at least [FOUNDER NOMINATION THRESHOLD]% of the issued and outstanding shares, shall have the right to nominate all directors of the Corporation.

(d) Following a Qualified Financing, the parties shall negotiate in good faith to provide for Investor board representation as appropriate.

### 2.2 Board Meetings

(a) The Board shall meet at least quarterly, or more frequently as determined by the Board.

(b) Board meetings may be held in person, by telephone, or by video conference.

(c) A quorum for Board meetings shall be a majority of directors.

### 2.3 Shareholder Meetings

(a) An annual meeting of shareholders shall be held within six (6) months of each fiscal year end.

(b) Special meetings may be called by the Board or by shareholders holding at least [SPECIAL MEETING THRESHOLD]% of the issued shares.

### 2.4 Reserved Matters – Board Approval

The following matters require the prior approval of the Board:

(a) Any acquisition, merger, or amalgamation involving the Corporation;

(b) Any sale, lease, or disposition of assets outside the ordinary course of business with a value exceeding CAD $[ASSET DISPOSITION THRESHOLD];

(c) Entry into any contract or commitment with a value exceeding CAD $[CONTRACT THRESHOLD];

(d) Any borrowing or incurrence of indebtedness exceeding CAD $[DEBT THRESHOLD];

(e) The hiring or termination of any executive officer;

(f) Any changes to employee compensation exceeding CAD $[COMPENSATION THRESHOLD] annually;

(g) Approval of the annual operating budget;

(h) Any related-party transactions; and

(i) Commencement or settlement of any material litigation.

### 2.5 Reserved Matters – Shareholder Approval

The following matters require the prior approval of Shareholders holding at least Supermajority Approval, and no such action shall be taken by the Corporation without such approval:

(a) Any amendment to the Articles or By-Laws of the Corporation;

(b) Any increase or decrease in the authorized share capital or creation of new classes or series of shares;

(c) Any issuance of Equity Securities (except for issuances pursuant to an approved employee equity incentive plan);

(d) Any declaration or payment of dividends;

(e) Any Change of Control or Sale Transaction;

(f) Any IPO;

(g) Any voluntary dissolution, liquidation, or winding-up of the Corporation;

(h) Any amendment, modification, or termination of this Agreement;

(i) Any transaction with a Shareholder or an Affiliate of a Shareholder exceeding CAD $[RELATED PARTY THRESHOLD], other than compensation arrangements approved by the Board; and

(j) The adoption, modification, or termination of any employee equity incentive plan.

(a) Amend the rights, preferences, or privileges of the Common Shares in a manner that adversely affects [MINORITY SHAREHOLDER ROLE] disproportionately relative to other holders of Common Shares;

(b) Increase the size of the Board to more than [MAXIMUM DIRECTORS] directors;

(c) Enter into any transaction with the Founder or an Affiliate of the Founder that is not on arm's length terms; or

(d) Make any material change to the nature of the Corporation's business.

## Article 3 – Restriction on Directors' Powers

### 3.1 Unanimous Shareholder Agreement

In accordance with Section 146 of the Act, the Shareholders hereby agree that, to the extent provided in this Agreement, the powers of the directors to manage, or supervise the management of, the business and affairs of the Corporation are restricted, and the Shareholders shall have and may exercise all such powers to the extent so restricted.

### 3.2 Liability

To the extent that this Agreement restricts the powers of the directors, the Shareholders who are given those powers shall have all the liabilities of a director under the Act.

## Article 4 – Restrictions on Transfer of Shares

### 4.1 General Restriction

No Shareholder shall Transfer any Shares except:

(a) In accordance with the provisions of this Article 4;

(b) Pursuant to a Drag-Along under Article 5;

(c) Pursuant to a Tag-Along under Article 5; or

(d) As otherwise approved in writing by all other Shareholders.

### 4.2 Prohibition on Encumbrance

No Shareholder shall pledge, hypothecate, or otherwise encumber any Shares without the prior written consent of the Majority Shareholders.

### 4.3 Right of First Refusal

(a) Before any Shareholder (the "Transferring Shareholder") Transfers any Shares to a third party (other than a Permitted Transfer), the Transferring Shareholder shall first deliver written notice (the "ROFR Notice") to the Corporation and the other Shareholders specifying:

   (i) The number and class of Shares proposed to be Transferred;

   (ii) The identity of the proposed transferee;

   (iii) The proposed purchase price and other material terms of the Transfer; and

   (iv) Evidence that the proposed transferee has made a bona fide offer in writing.

(b) Within thirty (30) days after receipt of the ROFR Notice (the "ROFR Period"), the other Shareholders shall have the right, but not the obligation, to purchase all (but not less than all) of the Shares proposed to be Transferred, pro rata based on their respective shareholdings.

(c) If the other Shareholders do not exercise their right of first refusal in full within the ROFR Period, the Corporation shall have the right, but not the obligation, to purchase any remaining Shares at the same price and on the same terms.

(d) If neither the Shareholders nor the Corporation exercise their rights in full, the Transferring Shareholder may complete the Transfer to the third party on terms no more favourable to the transferee than those set forth in the ROFR Notice, provided that:

   (i) The Transfer is completed within sixty (60) days after the expiration of the ROFR Period;

   (ii) The transferee agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement; and

   (iii) The Transfer complies with all applicable securities laws.

### 4.4 Permitted Transfers

The following Transfers are "Permitted Transfers" and shall not trigger the right of first refusal:

(a) Transfers by a Shareholder who is an individual to:

   (i) A Family Member of such Shareholder;

   (ii) A trust for the benefit of such Shareholder or Family Members; or

   (iii) A holding corporation wholly owned by such Shareholder;

(b) Transfers by a Shareholder that is a corporation to any Affiliate; and

(c) Transfers upon the death of a Shareholder to such Shareholder's estate, executor, or heirs;

**provided that**, in each case:

   (i) The transferee agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement;

   (ii) No consideration is paid (other than in the case of transfers to a holding corporation); and

   (iii) The Transferring Shareholder notifies the Corporation and other Shareholders in writing within ten (10) days of the Transfer.

### 4.5 Legend on Share Certificates

All share certificates shall bear the following legend:

> "THE SHARES REPRESENTED BY THIS CERTIFICATE ARE SUBJECT TO THE RESTRICTIONS ON TRANSFER SET FORTH IN A UNANIMOUS SHAREHOLDERS AGREEMENT DATED [USA DATE], AS AMENDED FROM TIME TO TIME. A COPY OF SUCH AGREEMENT IS ON FILE AT THE REGISTERED OFFICE OF THE CORPORATION."

### 4.6 No Transfer Unless Compliant

The Corporation shall not register any Transfer of Shares on its books unless such Transfer is made in compliance with this Agreement.

### 4.7 Fair Market Value Determination

If the parties cannot agree on Fair Market Value for any purpose under this Agreement:

(a) The parties shall jointly appoint an independent valuator within fifteen (15) days;

(b) If they cannot agree on a valuator, each party shall nominate one valuator, and those two valuators shall select a third, who shall conduct the valuation;

(c) The valuator shall determine Fair Market Value using standard valuation methodologies appropriate for a private company, including consideration of comparable transactions, discounted cash flows, and asset values;

(d) The valuator's determination shall be final and binding; and

(e) The costs of the valuator shall be borne equally by the parties unless the valuator determines otherwise.

## Article 5 – Drag-Along and Tag-Along Rights

### 5.1 Drag-Along Rights

(a) If Shareholders holding at least 66⅔% of the issued and outstanding shares (the "Dragging Shareholders") approve a bona fide sale of all or substantially all of the issued and outstanding Shares to a third party purchaser (a "Sale Transaction"), the Dragging Shareholders may require all other Shareholders to sell all of their Shares to such purchaser (the "Drag-Along Buyer") on the same terms and conditions.

(b) The Dragging Shareholders shall deliver written notice (the "Drag-Along Notice") to all other Shareholders at least thirty (30) days prior to the proposed closing, specifying:

   (i) The identity of the Drag-Along Buyer;

   (ii) The purchase price per Share and other material terms; and

   (iii) The proposed closing date.

(c) Each Shareholder receiving a Drag-Along Notice shall:

   (i) Sell all of their Shares at the same price per Share and on the same terms;

   (ii) Execute all documents reasonably required to complete the Sale Transaction;

   (iii) Make representations and warranties regarding title to their Shares and authority to sell; and

   (iv) Participate on a pro rata basis in any escrow, holdback, indemnification, or purchase price adjustment.

(d) No Shareholder shall be required to:

   (i) Provide representations or warranties beyond those regarding title and authority;

   (ii) Agree to non-competition covenants (unless separately compensated); or

   (iii) Bear indemnification obligations in excess of their pro rata share of proceeds.

### 5.2 Tag-Along Rights

(a) If any Shareholder or group of Shareholders proposes to Transfer Shares representing more than [TAG-ALONG THRESHOLD]% of the issued and outstanding shares to a third party (a "Tag-Along Sale"), such selling Shareholder(s) shall first give written notice (the "Tag-Along Notice") to all other Shareholders at least thirty (30) days prior to the proposed closing, specifying:

   (i) The identity of the proposed purchaser;

   (ii) The number of Shares to be sold;

   (iii) The purchase price per Share and other material terms; and

   (iv) The proposed closing date.

(b) Each other Shareholder (a "Tagging Shareholder") shall have the right, but not the obligation, to participate in such sale on the same terms, by delivering written notice within twenty (20) days of receipt of the Tag-Along Notice.

(c) If one or more Shareholders exercise their tag-along rights:

   (i) The Shares to be sold to the purchaser shall be allocated pro rata among all selling Shareholders (including Tagging Shareholders); and

   (ii) If the purchaser is unwilling to purchase all Shares offered by all selling Shareholders, the selling Shareholder(s) shall reduce their sale accordingly.

### 5.3 Termination of Drag-Along and Tag-Along

The drag-along and tag-along rights under this Article 5 shall terminate upon the earlier of:

(a) An IPO; or

(b) A Change of Control.

## Article 6 – Pre-Emptive Rights

### 6.1 Right to Participate in New Issuances

(a) Before the Corporation issues any Equity Securities (other than Excluded Securities) (the "New Securities"), the Corporation shall first offer each Shareholder the right to purchase their Pro Rata Share of such New Securities on the same terms.

(b) "Excluded Securities" means:

   (i) Shares issued pursuant to an employee equity incentive plan approved by the Board;

   (ii) Shares issued upon conversion or exercise of convertible securities or options outstanding as of the date hereof or subsequently approved;

   (iii) Shares issued in connection with an IPO;

   (iv) Shares issued in connection with a strategic partnership or acquisition approved by the Board; and

   (v) Shares issued for consideration other than cash (such as services or IP assignments).

### 6.2 Procedure

(a) The Corporation shall deliver written notice to each Shareholder at least thirty (30) days before the proposed issuance, specifying:

   (i) The number and class of New Securities to be issued;

   (ii) The price per security and other material terms;

   (iii) The identity of any proposed third-party investor(s); and

   (iv) The deadline for exercising pre-emptive rights.

(b) Each Shareholder shall have twenty (20) days to exercise their pre-emptive right by delivering written notice to the Corporation.

(c) If any Shareholder does not exercise their pre-emptive right in full, the other Shareholders may purchase the unsubscribed portion pro rata.

### 6.3 Waiver

Pre-emptive rights may be waived by written consent of Shareholders holding at least Supermajority Approval.

## Article 7 – Information Rights

### 7.1 Financial Information

The Corporation shall provide each Shareholder with:

(a) Audited annual financial statements (or reviewed statements if an audit is not required) within one hundred twenty (120) days after fiscal year end;

(b) Unaudited quarterly financial statements within forty-five (45) days after each quarter end;

(c) An annual operating budget at least thirty (30) days before each fiscal year; and

(d) Such other financial and operational information as reasonably requested.

### 7.2 Capitalization Information

The Corporation shall maintain and provide upon request:

(a) An up-to-date capitalization table;

(b) A copy of the securities register; and

(c) Copies of all share certificates and related documentation.

### 7.3 Inspection Rights

Each Shareholder shall have the right to inspect the books, records, and minute books of the Corporation during normal business hours upon reasonable notice.

### 7.4 Confidentiality of Information

All information provided pursuant to this Article 7 shall be subject to the confidentiality obligations set forth in Article 9.

## Article 8 – Intellectual Property and Business Protection

### 8.1 IP Assignment Acknowledgment

Each Shareholder acknowledges that:

(a) All intellectual property created by or on behalf of the Corporation is the sole property of the Corporation;

(b) Each Shareholder who is also an employee, contractor, or service provider of the Corporation shall execute and comply with an IP and Confidentiality Agreement; and

(c) The Corporation's intellectual property is a material asset and the Shareholders shall not take any action to impair such intellectual property.

### 8.2 Non-Competition

(a) During the term of this Agreement and for a period of [NON-COMPETE MONTHS] months following the date on which a Shareholder ceases to hold any Shares (or ceases to be an employee or director of the Corporation, if later), such Shareholder shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in, own, operate, or provide services to any business that competes directly with the business of the Corporation in Canada or any other jurisdiction where the Corporation operates.

(b) This Section 8.2 shall not apply to:

   (i) Ownership of less than 5% of publicly traded securities;

   (ii) Shareholders who cease to hold Shares as a result of an involuntary Transfer or a sale pursuant to Article 5; or

   (iii) Activities approved in writing by the Board.

### 8.3 Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of [NON-SOLICIT MONTHS] months thereafter, no Shareholder shall, directly or indirectly:

(a) Solicit, induce, or attempt to solicit or induce any employee, contractor, or consultant of the Corporation to leave their position; or

(b) Solicit or divert any customer, client, or supplier of the Corporation.

### 8.4 Reasonableness

The Shareholders acknowledge that the covenants in Sections 8.2 and 8.3 are reasonable and necessary to protect the legitimate business interests of the Corporation.

## Article 9 – Confidentiality

### 9.1 Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to the Corporation, its business, operations, technology, customers, suppliers, financial condition, and prospects, including:

(a) Trade secrets, know-how, and proprietary technology;

(b) Business plans, strategies, and financial projections;

(c) Customer and supplier lists and contracts;

(d) Employee information and compensation; and

(e) The terms of this Agreement.

### 9.2 Confidentiality Obligations

Each Shareholder agrees to:

(a) Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential;

(b) Use Confidential Information solely for purposes related to their shareholding;

(c) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent; and

(d) Take reasonable measures to protect Confidential Information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

### 9.3 Permitted Disclosures

Confidential Information may be disclosed:

(a) To a Shareholder's legal, financial, or tax advisors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations;

(b) To a prospective purchaser of Shares in connection with a Transfer permitted by this Agreement, subject to a confidentiality agreement;

(c) As required by law, court order, or regulatory authority; or

(d) With the prior written consent of the Corporation.

### 9.4 Return of Information

Upon ceasing to be a Shareholder, a Person shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information in their possession.

## Article 10 – Qualified Financing and Future Investors

### 10.1 Qualified Financing

Upon completion of a Qualified Financing:

(a) The parties agree to negotiate in good faith amendments to this Agreement as reasonably required by the Investors;

(b) The Corporation shall have the right to create new classes or series of shares with such rights, preferences, and privileges as may be agreed with the Investors; and

(c) The Shareholders shall cooperate in good faith to complete the Qualified Financing.

### 10.2 Investor Rights

The parties acknowledge that future Investors may require:

(a) Board observer or director nomination rights;

(b) Enhanced information rights;

(c) Anti-dilution protection;

(d) Liquidation preferences; and

(e) Other customary investor protections.

The Shareholders agree to consider such requests in good faith.

### 10.3 Amendments for Financing

Any amendment to this Agreement made in connection with a Qualified Financing shall require only Supermajority Approval (rather than unanimous consent), provided that no amendment shall:

(a) Adversely affect any Shareholder disproportionately relative to other holders of the same class without such Shareholder's consent; or

(b) Remove or materially reduce the protective provisions in Section 2.6 without the affected Shareholder's consent.

## Article 11 – IPO and Exit

### 11.1 IPO

(a) The Corporation may pursue an IPO upon approval by the Board and Supermajority Approval of the Shareholders.

(b) Upon completion of an IPO, the following provisions of this Agreement shall automatically terminate:

   (i) Article 4 (Transfer Restrictions), except for any contractual lock-up period;

   (ii) Article 5 (Drag-Along and Tag-Along);

   (iii) Article 6 (Pre-Emptive Rights); and

   (iv) Article 7 (Information Rights), to the extent the Corporation is subject to public disclosure requirements.

(c) Each Shareholder agrees to execute any customary lock-up agreement required by the underwriters in connection with an IPO.

### 11.2 Sale of the Corporation

In the event of a Change of Control:

(a) The proceeds shall be distributed among Shareholders pro rata based on their shareholdings;

(b) Each Shareholder shall execute documents reasonably required to complete the transaction; and

(c) This Agreement shall terminate upon closing of the Change of Control.

## Article 12 – Dispute Resolution

### 12.1 Negotiation

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall first be addressed through good faith negotiation between the parties for a period of thirty (30) days.

### 12.2 Mediation

If the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation, the parties shall submit the dispute to mediation in [CITY], [PROVINCE], before a mediator mutually agreed upon by the parties.

### 12.3 Arbitration

If mediation is unsuccessful within sixty (60) days, the dispute shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration in [CITY], [PROVINCE], in accordance with the Arbitration Act, 1991 ([PROVINCE]). The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator with experience in corporate and commercial matters. The decision of the arbitrator shall be final and binding on the parties.

### 12.4 Injunctive Relief

Notwithstanding the foregoing, any party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief from a court of competent jurisdiction to prevent irreparable harm, including enforcement of the confidentiality, non-competition, and non-solicitation provisions of this Agreement.

### 12.5 Costs

Unless otherwise ordered by the arbitrator, each party shall bear its own costs and an equal share of the costs of the arbitration.

## Article 13 – Term and Termination

### 13.1 Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect until the earliest of:

(a) The written agreement of all parties to terminate;

(b) Completion of an IPO;

(c) Completion of a Change of Control; or

(d) Only one Person remains as a Shareholder.

### 13.2 Survival

The following provisions shall survive termination of this Agreement:

(a) Article 9 (Confidentiality);

(b) Section 8.2 (Non-Competition), for the period specified therein;

(c) Section 8.3 (Non-Solicitation), for the period specified therein; and

(d) Article 12 (Dispute Resolution).

## Article 14 – General Provisions

### 14.1 Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the Articles, By-Laws, and any schedules hereto, constitutes the entire agreement among the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and negotiations.

### 14.2 Amendments

Except as otherwise provided herein, this Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by all parties.

### 14.3 Waiver

No waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing. The failure to enforce any provision shall not constitute a waiver thereof.

### 14.4 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

### 14.6 Notices

All notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, registered mail, or email to the addresses set forth above (or such other address as a party may specify in writing). Notices shall be deemed received: (a) if delivered by hand or courier, upon delivery; (b) if by registered mail, five (5) Business Days after mailing; or (c) if by email, upon confirmation of receipt.

### 14.7 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Province of [PROVINCE] and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.

### 14.8 Counterparts and Electronic Execution

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. Signatures may be delivered by electronic means (including PDF, DocuSign, or similar) and shall be as valid as original signatures.

### 14.9 Further Assurances

Each party shall execute and deliver such further documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to give effect to this Agreement.

### 14.10 Independent Legal Advice

Each party acknowledges that they have been advised to obtain independent legal advice regarding this Agreement and have either obtained such advice or voluntarily chosen not to do so.

### 14.11 Binding on Corporation

The Corporation hereby agrees to be bound by this Agreement and to observe and give effect to its provisions.
