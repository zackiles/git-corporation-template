## Annual Filing Requirements

As an extra-provincial corporation registered in Ontario, the Corporation must file an **Annual Return** with ServiceOntario:

- **Due Date:** Within 60 days after the anniversary of the date of incorporation or amalgamation
- **Filing Method:** Ontario Business Registry (online)
- **Fee:** Varies (check current rates at ServiceOntario)
