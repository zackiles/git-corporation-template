## Resolutions

**NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:**

### 1. Adoption of By-Law No. 1

By-Law No. 1, in the form attached hereto or otherwise presented to this meeting, being a general by-law regulating the business and affairs of the Corporation, is hereby enacted as a by-law of the Corporation, subject to confirmation by the shareholders.

### 2. Confirmation of Registered Office

The registered office of the Corporation is confirmed at:

**[REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS]**

### 3. Appointment of Officers

The following individuals are hereby appointed as officers of the Corporation to hold office at the pleasure of the Board:

| Name | Position(s) |
|------|-------------|
| [NAME] | [President, Chief Executive Officer, Secretary, Treasurer, etc.] |

### 4. Fiscal Year End

The fiscal year end of the Corporation is confirmed as **[FISCAL YEAR END DATE]**.

### 5. Authorization to Issue Founder Shares

The Board hereby authorizes the issuance of **[NUMBER] ([NUMBER IN WORDS]) [SHARE CLASS] shares** to [FOUNDER NAME] (the "Founder") for a total consideration of **$[AMOUNT] CAD** (representing $[PRICE PER SHARE] per share), such shares to be fully paid and non-assessable upon receipt of the consideration.

The consideration shall be paid by the Founder upon or prior to issuance, and upon receipt thereof, Share Certificate No. [CERT NUMBER] shall be issued to the Founder.

### 6. Form of Share Certificate

The form of share certificate presented to this meeting, or a substantially similar form, is hereby approved for use by the Corporation. The Corporation may also issue shares in uncertificated form, with written confirmation provided to the shareholder.

### 7. Registers

The Secretary is directed to establish and maintain the following registers at the registered office of the Corporation (or at such other location in Canada as the Board may designate):

- Register of Directors
- Register of Officers
- Register of Shareholders
- Securities Register
- Register of Share Transfers

### 8. Banking Resolution

The Corporation is authorized to open and maintain one or more bank accounts with any chartered bank or credit union in Canada selected by the officers, and the following persons are authorized to sign cheques, drafts, orders, and other negotiable instruments on behalf of the Corporation:

| Name | Signing Authority |
|------|-------------------|
| [NAME] | [e.g., Any single signature up to unlimited amount] |

The officers are authorized to execute any banking documents, resolutions, or agreements required by the financial institution.

### 9. Books and Records

The books and records of the Corporation, including the securities register, minute book, and accounting records, shall be kept at the registered office or at such other place in Canada as the Board may determine.

### 10. Auditor

Given that the Corporation is a "non-distributing corporation" and all shareholders consent, the Corporation is exempt from the requirement to appoint an auditor pursuant to Section 163 of the Canada Business Corporations Act. The shareholders may revisit this determination at any annual meeting.

### 11. Professional Advisors

The officers are authorized to engage accountants, lawyers, and other professional advisors as necessary for the proper conduct of the Corporation's business.

### 12. Extra-Provincial Registration

The officers are authorized and directed to register the Corporation as an extra-provincial corporation in the Province of [PROVINCE] and in any other province or territory in which the Corporation carries on business, and to execute all documents and take all actions necessary to complete such registrations.

### 13. Business Number and Tax Registrations

The officers are authorized to apply for a Business Number with the Canada Revenue Agency and to register for GST/HST, payroll, and any other applicable federal or provincial tax accounts.

### 14. General Authority

Any director or officer of the Corporation is hereby authorized to execute and deliver any and all documents and instruments and to do any and all acts and things as may be necessary or desirable to give effect to the foregoing resolutions.
