## Resolution

**NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:**

### 1. General Authorization to Issue Shares

The Board of Directors is hereby authorized to allot and issue, from time to time, [SHARE CLASS] shares of the Corporation to such persons, in such numbers, and for such consideration as the Board may determine in its sole discretion, subject to:
- The Articles of Incorporation
- The Canada Business Corporations Act
- Any applicable securities laws
- Any Unanimous Shareholder Agreement that may be in effect

### 2. Fair Market Value

For any share issuance, the Board shall determine the fair market value of the shares at the time of issuance having regard to:
- Any recent arm's length transactions involving the Corporation's shares
- The Corporation's net asset value
- The Corporation's earnings and cash flow (if any)
- The Corporation's prospects and stage of development
- Comparable company valuations
- Such other factors as the Board considers relevant

### 3. Form of Consideration

Shares may be issued for consideration in money, property, or past services, as permitted by the Act. The Board shall determine the value of any non-cash consideration.

### 4. Procedures for Future Issuances

For each future share issuance, the following procedures shall be followed:

1. **Board Resolution:** A specific resolution shall be passed authorizing the particular issuance, specifying:
   - The subscriber(s)
   - Number and class of shares
   - Price per share and total consideration
   - Form of consideration
   - Any restrictions or conditions

2. **Documentation:** Appropriate documentation shall be prepared, including:
   - Share Subscription Agreement (where applicable)
   - Share Certificate or confirmation of uncertificated shares
   - Updates to the Securities Register and Shareholders Register

3. **Compliance:** The Secretary shall ensure compliance with:
   - Securities law exemptions (as applicable)
   - Any pre-emptive rights (if established)
   - Any Unanimous Shareholder Agreement provisions

### 5. Equity Incentive Pool

The Board is authorized to reserve up to [ESOP PERCENTAGE]% of the fully-diluted share capital for issuance under equity incentive plans, including:
- Stock options
- Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)
- Other equity-based compensation

Any such plan shall be established by separate resolution of the Board and, where required, approved by shareholders.

### 6. Pre-Emptive Rights

Until such time as a Unanimous Shareholder Agreement or other agreement provides otherwise, existing shareholders shall have no pre-emptive rights to acquire additional shares upon any new issuance.

### 7. Reporting

The Secretary shall maintain accurate records of all share issuances and shall report to the Board at least annually on the Corporation's capitalization.
