## Article 1 – Interpretation

### 1.1 Definitions

In this by-law:

- **"Act"** means the Canada Business Corporations Act and regulations thereunder, as amended.
- **"Articles"** means the articles of incorporation of the Corporation, as amended.
- **"Board"** means the board of directors of the Corporation.
- **"Corporation"** means [CORPORATION NAME].
- **"Director"** means a director of the Corporation.
- **"Officer"** means an officer of the Corporation.
- **"Shareholder"** means a shareholder of the Corporation.

## Article 3 – Directors

### 3.1 Number

The Board shall consist of a minimum of [MINIMUM NUMBER] and a maximum of [MAXIMUM NUMBER] directors, as determined from time to time by special resolution of the shareholders.

### 3.2 Qualifications

A director need not be a shareholder. At least twenty-five percent (25%) of the directors shall be resident Canadians, as required by the Act.

### 3.3 Election and Term

Directors shall be elected at each annual meeting of shareholders and shall hold office until the next annual meeting or until their successors are elected or appointed, unless a director resigns or is removed earlier.

### 3.4 Removal

Shareholders may remove a director by ordinary resolution at a special meeting called for that purpose, or by written resolution signed by all shareholders entitled to vote on such resolution.

### 3.5 Vacancies

Subject to the Act, a vacancy on the Board may be filled by resolution of the remaining directors, provided that the remaining directors constitute a quorum. A director so appointed holds office until the next annual meeting of shareholders.

### 3.6 Powers

The Board shall manage or supervise the management of the business and affairs of the Corporation. The Board may exercise all powers that are not required by the Act or the Articles to be exercised by the shareholders.

### 3.7 Standard of Care

Every director and officer shall, in exercising their powers and discharging their duties:
- Act honestly and in good faith with a view to the best interests of the Corporation; and
- Exercise the care, diligence, and skill that a reasonably prudent person would exercise in comparable circumstances.

---

## Article 4 – Officers

### 4.1 Appointment

The Board may appoint officers including a President, Chief Executive Officer, Secretary, Treasurer, Chief Financial Officer, Chief Technology Officer, and such other officers as the Board determines. Any two or more offices may be held by the same person.

### 4.2 Duties

Officers shall have such duties as the Board may prescribe or as are incident to their office. In the absence of specific direction by the Board:

- **President/Chief Executive Officer:** General supervision and management of the business and affairs of the Corporation
- **Secretary:** Custody of corporate records, minutes, and seal (if any); issuance of notices
- **Treasurer/Chief Financial Officer:** Custody of funds and securities; maintenance of financial records

### 4.3 Removal

The Board may remove any officer at any time, with or without cause.

### 4.4 Delegation

Officers may delegate any of their powers to other officers or employees, subject to approval by the Board.

---

## Article 5 – Meetings of Directors

### 5.1 Place of Meetings

Meetings of the Board may be held at any place within or outside Canada as the Board may determine. Directors may participate by telephone or other communication facilities that permit all participants to communicate adequately with each other.

### 5.2 Calling Meetings

Meetings of the Board may be called by the Chair, the President, or any two directors.

### 5.3 Notice

Notice of meetings shall be given at least forty-eight (48) hours before the meeting by mail, email, telephone, or other means. No notice is required if all directors are present and none objects to the holding of the meeting, or if all directors consent in writing to the meeting being held without notice.

### 5.4 Quorum

A majority of the directors constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business. Where the Corporation has only one director, that director constitutes a quorum.

### 5.5 Voting

Each director has one vote. Decisions are made by majority vote of directors present at a meeting at which a quorum is present. In case of an equality of votes, the chair of the meeting does not have a second or casting vote.

### 5.6 Resolutions in Writing

A resolution in writing signed by all directors entitled to vote on that resolution is as valid as if passed at a meeting of the Board.

---

## Article 6 – Meetings of Shareholders

### 6.1 Annual Meetings

The annual meeting of shareholders shall be held within six (6) months after the end of each fiscal year at such time and place as the Board determines.

### 6.2 Special Meetings

Special meetings of shareholders may be called by the Board, or by shareholders holding at least five percent (5%) of the issued shares entitled to vote at the meeting.

### 6.3 Notice

Notice of meetings shall be given at least twenty-one (21) days but not more than sixty (60) days before the meeting to each shareholder entitled to vote, to each director, and to the auditor (if any).

### 6.4 Quorum

Shareholders holding at least [QUORUM PERCENTAGE]% of the shares entitled to vote, present in person or represented by proxy, constitute a quorum.

### 6.5 Voting

Each share entitled to vote carries one vote. Decisions are made by ordinary resolution (majority vote) unless a special resolution (two-thirds majority) is required by the Act, the Articles, or this by-law.

### 6.6 Proxies

A shareholder may appoint a proxy holder to attend and act at a meeting in their stead. A proxy shall be in writing and signed by the shareholder or their attorney.

### 6.7 Resolutions in Writing

A resolution in writing signed by all shareholders entitled to vote on that resolution is as valid as if passed at a meeting of shareholders.

### 6.8 Participation by Electronic Means

Shareholders may participate in meetings by telephone or other communication facilities that permit all participants to communicate adequately with each other, unless the Articles or the notice of meeting provides otherwise.

---

## Article 7 – Banking and Borrowing

### 7.1 Banking

The Board shall designate the bankers of the Corporation by resolution and shall authorize one or more officers or other persons to sign cheques, drafts, or other negotiable instruments on behalf of the Corporation.

### 7.2 Borrowing

Subject to the Articles and any unanimous shareholder agreement, the Board may, without authorization of the shareholders:
- Borrow money on the credit of the Corporation
- Issue, reissue, sell, or pledge debt obligations of the Corporation
- Mortgage, hypothecate, pledge, or otherwise create a security interest in all or any property of the Corporation to secure any debt obligation

### 7.3 Delegation of Borrowing Powers

The Board may delegate any of the borrowing powers set out above to a director, committee of directors, or officer of the Corporation.

---

## Article 8 – Dividends

### 8.1 Declaration

The Board may declare and the Corporation may pay dividends to shareholders according to their respective rights and interests.

### 8.2 Solvency Test

No dividend shall be declared or paid if there are reasonable grounds for believing that:
- The Corporation is, or would after the payment be, unable to pay its liabilities as they become due; or
- The realizable value of the Corporation's assets would thereby be less than the aggregate of its liabilities and stated capital of all classes.

### 8.3 Stock Dividends

The Board may declare stock dividends payable in shares of the Corporation.

---

## Article 9 – Shares and Share Certificates

### 9.1 Issue of Shares

Shares may be issued at such times, to such persons, for such consideration, and on such terms as the Board determines, subject to the Act, the Articles, and any unanimous shareholder agreement.

### 9.2 Share Certificates

Share certificates shall be in a form approved by the Board and shall be signed by at least one director or officer. The Corporation may issue shares without certificates, in which case the Corporation shall provide written notice of issuance to the shareholder.

### 9.3 Transfer of Shares

Shares may be transferred by endorsement and delivery of the certificate, or by such other means as the Board may approve, subject to any restrictions in the Articles and to compliance with applicable securities laws.

### 9.4 Securities Register

The Corporation shall maintain a securities register at its registered office or at such other place in Canada as the Board may designate.

---

## Article 10 – Indemnification

### 10.1 Indemnification of Directors and Officers

The Corporation shall indemnify a director or officer, a former director or officer, or another individual who acts or acted at the Corporation's request as a director or officer of another entity, against all costs, charges, and expenses, including any amount paid to settle an action or satisfy a judgment, reasonably incurred by them in respect of any civil, criminal, administrative, investigative, or other proceeding in which they are involved because of that association with the Corporation or other entity, if:
- The individual acted honestly and in good faith with a view to the best interests of the Corporation or other entity; and
- In the case of a criminal or administrative action or proceeding enforced by monetary penalty, the individual had reasonable grounds for believing that their conduct was lawful.

### 10.2 Advance of Costs

The Corporation may advance amounts to an individual for costs, charges, and expenses of a proceeding referred to above, provided that the individual shall repay such amounts if they do not fulfill the conditions set out above.

### 10.3 Insurance

The Corporation may purchase and maintain insurance for the benefit of any individual referred to above against any liability incurred by them in their capacity as a director or officer of the Corporation.

---

## Article 11 – Notices

### 11.1 Method

Notices to shareholders, directors, officers, or the auditor may be given personally, by mail, by fax, or by electronic means (including email).

### 11.2 Deemed Receipt

A notice given by mail is deemed received on the fifth day after mailing if mailed by prepaid mail in Canada. A notice given by email or other electronic means is deemed received when sent, if sent during normal business hours, or at the commencement of the next business day otherwise.

### 11.3 Shareholders Not Found

If a notice is returned undelivered, the Corporation is not required to send further notices until the shareholder provides a current address.

---

## Article 12 – Execution of Documents

### 12.1 Signing Authority

Documents requiring execution by the Corporation may be signed by any director or officer, or by such other persons as the Board may authorize by resolution.

### 12.2 Corporate Seal

The Corporation may, but is not required to, have a corporate seal. Documents may be executed with or without a seal.

### 12.3 Facsimile and Electronic Signatures

Signatures may be affixed by facsimile or electronic means, provided such signature is legally valid.
